How to Play

Each hexagon has a color and a number. One hexagon of each color is the neXus (the one with the X on it).

You can swap two hexagons by clicking one and then the other. The neXus cannot be moved.

When a hexagon's number matches its distance from the neXus of the same color, it will light up. The goal of the game is to have all hexagons light up at once. There may be more than one correct solution.

Settings can be adjusted beneath the grid. Currently they are set to a simple tutorial game with just two colors. Once you've got the hang of things, you can experiment with different grid sizes, more colors, or different grid styles.

Good luck and have fun!
