How to Play

Each hexagon has a color and a number. One hexagon of each color is the neXus (the one with the X on it).

You can swap two hexagons by clicking one and then the other. The neXus cannot be moved.

When a hexagon's number matches its distance from the neXus of the same color, it will light up. The goal of the game is to have all hexagons light up at once. There may be more than one correct solution.

Settings can be adjusted beneath the grid. Currently they are set to an easy tutorial game with just two colors. It is recommended to move up to three colors and increase the Difficulty to "Medium" once you understand the rules for a reasonable challenge, then change the Difficulty to "Hard" once you've really gotten the hang of the game. After that, feel free to start messing around with more complex grids.

Good luck and have fun!
